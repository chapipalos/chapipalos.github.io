PINMATCHBALL

A frenetic pinball game where each player controls 2 flippers to score goals against their opponent. First player to reach 9 points wins.

During the match, power-ups spawn across the arena, shaking up the gameplay in different ways. There are 4 types:

  • Freeze — Freezes the rival's flippers for a set amount of time.
  • Spike — Turns the ball into a spike ball. The next time it hits a rival flipper, that flipper is disabled for a set amount of time.
  • Teleport — Instantly teleports the ball back to your spawn position.
  • Ghost — For a limited time, the ball becomes a ghost and passes through all objects — including both your own and your rival's flippers.

The game features 3 game modes:

  • Local Multiplayer — Two players on the same computer.
  • Single Player — Play against an AI opponent.
  • Multiplayer — Play an online match against another player from anywhere in the world.

Dialogue Framework

A Unity asset for building 2D and 3D dialogue systems, designed to help developers prototype conversations and narrative interactions faster and with less boilerplate.

The framework provides a visual node editor for authoring branching dialogue trees, support for conditional logic, character portraits and event hooks that connect directly to Unity's component system. Built with flexibility in mind — it works for simple NPC lines as well as complex story-driven sequences, without locking you into any specific narrative structure.
